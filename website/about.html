About HabitBlock

We're on a mission to transform how people approach productivity, turning chaos into clarity through innovative visual planning tools.

Our Story

HabitBlock was born from a simple frustration: traditional planning tools were either too complex or too simple, never quite fitting how our minds actually work. We found ourselves constantly switching between apps, struggling with overcomplicated interfaces, and losing track of what truly mattered.

After years of research into productivity psychology and user behavior, we realized that the best planning happens visually. Your brain processes visual information 60,000 times faster than text - so why were all planning tools still text-based?

That's when we created HabitBlock: a visual planning experience that feels as natural as painting on a canvas, yet powerful enough to handle the complexity of modern life.
